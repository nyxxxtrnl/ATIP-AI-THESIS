Gentle Wellness Suggestions

Small steps you can try at your own pace.

  • Take a mindful pause. Breathe slowly and notice how you feel, without judging yourself.
  • Connect with someone you trust. A simple conversation can help you feel supported.
  • Make room for rest. Choose one calming activity, like stretching, listening to music, or stepping outside.

These are general wellness ideas, not a substitute for professional mental health care.
